# Practice sentences, one per line, grouped under "## <focus>" headings.
# tools/make_sentences.py turns this into app/src/main/assets/sentences.json
# with the native General American phonemes for every word.

## TH (think, this)
I think there are three of them.
They thought this was the thirtieth.
Both brothers were there that Thursday.
Thank you for the birthday breakfast.
The weather is better than they said.

## American R
The red car is around the corner.
Our rare bird is very early.
Rory wrote a really short report.
Turn right after the third tree.
Her order arrived on Thursday morning.

## L and dark L
Little Lily likes yellow lemons.
Please fill the bottle with cold milk.
The hall was full of tall people.
Lucy will call you later.

## V and W
We visited a very wide valley.
Victor waved when the van went by.
Would you leave the window open?
The wolf was very wary of the village.

## Short i and long ee
Sit in this seat, please.
He will leave in a minute.
The ship is cheap but the sheep is not.
Fill the green bin with leaves.

## Cat vowel (æ)
That black cat sat on my hat.
Ask Sam to pass the jam.
The man had a bad map.
Can you hand me that bag?

## Cup vowel (ʌ) and cot vowel (ɑ)
My brother lost his gloves on Sunday.
Come up and have some lunch.
Bob got a hot coffee from the shop.
The cup is in the box on top.

## Glides (ay, oh)
Take the late train to Spain.
Go home and close the window slowly.
They may stay a day or so.
Don't throw the phone in the boat.

## Flap T (water, better)
Put a little water in the kettle.
I'd rather get a better city map.
Peter bought a lot of butter.
What a pretty little kitty.

## Final consonants
I asked him to fix the desk.
She helped them build the bridge.
The kids played in the cold wind.
He looked at the old maps.

## H and NG
His house is behind the hill.
How long have you been singing?
The king is bringing something.
Harry hopes to have a holiday.

## Everyday sentences
Could you tell me where the station is?
I would like a cup of coffee, please.
It's nice to meet you.
What time does the meeting start?
I'm going to the store this afternoon.
